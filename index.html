03 MANUFACTURING & QUALITY ASSURANCE

Where precision meets
craftsmanship.

Glass forming, careful annealing, and detailed inspection shape every piece of Biohall labware. Confidence in every measurement. Our quality approach considers material reliability, dimensional precision, and readable graduations.

01 / MATERIAL

Material reliability

Borosilicate 3.3, selected for thermal and chemical resistance.

02 / FORM⌖

Glass forming

Automatic and semi-automatic production, supported by skilled glassworking.

03 / STRENGTHEN↗

Annealing

Controlled annealing helps relieve internal stress in the finished glassware.

04 / VERIFY✳

Quality checks

Dimensional, joint-fit, and print checks bring quality into focus. Wall thickness and dimensions checked against technical requirements. Printing checked through production for clear, dependable markings.
