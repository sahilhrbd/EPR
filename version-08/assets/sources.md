## Map

world-map.svg is an equirectangular rendering of country geometry from [world.geo.json](https://github.com/johan/world.geo.json), based on [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) public-domain map data. Antarctica omitted. Only India is highlighted. Its refined outline uses the [DataMeet composite India boundary](https://github.com/datameet/maps/blob/master/Country/README.md) (CC0), including Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh in the Survey of India depiction. The geometry is lightly simplified at subpixel scale for this SVG. Delhi marker uses 28.6139° N, 77.2090° E; it is a geographic focus, not a new office-address claim.
